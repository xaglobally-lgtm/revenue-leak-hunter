### 1. Services

RLH Provider will inspect Client's billing system (currently read-only access to
Client's Stripe account, and any other platforms both parties agree to) to
identify revenue leaks — including but not limited to failed or delinquent
recurring payments, unbilled usage, under-billed seats, billing-mismatches, and
price/plan discrepancies. RLH Provider will document each finding with evidence,
recommend fixes, and help Client verify and collect the resulting revenue.

### 2. Compensation — pure contingency, 10%

2.1 Client pays RLH Provider **ten percent (10%)** of the **cash actually
received** by Client that is (a) attributable to a finding RLH Provider
identified, and (b) collected within the attribution window in Section 2.3.

2.2 **No fees upfront.** Client owes nothing for finding potential, rejected
findings, or unrealized amounts. RLH Provider earns only when Client actually
collects money.

2.3 **Attribution window:** a finding is attributable if the cash is received
within **12 months** after RLH Provider first presents that finding to Client in
writing (in the app's opportunity detail or a report).

2.4 Client keeps **90%** of every recovered dollar. Nothing is deducted from
recoveries at the payment source; RLH Provider invoices its 10% separately.

### 3. Payment of the 10% fee

3.1 RLH Provider will provide a **Client Fee Statement** generated from the app
showing each recovered payment, the 10% fee, and net client amount.

3.2 Client shall pay each invoice within **[15 / 30] days** of the statement
date, via ACH, wire, or card to RLH Provider's account.

3.3 Client owes the fee only for cash actually received (Section 2.1). No fee on
amounts Client forgives, waives, or fails to collect.

### 4. Access and independence

4.1 RLH Provider connects to Client's billing data using **read-only** access
(restricted API keys). RLH Provider never receives, processes, or stores Client's
payment funds; all collected revenue goes directly to Client's own accounts.

4.2 RLH Provider is an **independent contractor**, not an employee, partner, or
agent of Client. Neither party may bind the other without prior written consent.

### 5. Confidentiality and data

5.1 Both parties will keep non-public business, financial, and customer data
confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement.

5.2 Client data is handled under RLH Provider's privacy practices; both parties
will comply with applicable data-protection laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

5.3 Upon termination, RLH Provider will delete or return Client's data as Client
reasonably requests.

### 6. Term and termination

6.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and continues until terminated by
either party with **[14 / 30] days** written notice.

6.2 Amounts earned before termination (recoveries already received) remain due.
Findings presented before termination keep their 12-month attribution window
until it expires.

### 7. Dispute resolution and law

7.1 Governing law: **[STATE / COUNTRY]**.
7.2 Disputes shall first be resolved in good-faith negotiation, then (if not
resolved) via binding arbitration or small-claims court in **[PLACE]**, as the
parties may choose.
7.3 Either party may enforce this Agreement in any court of competent
jurisdiction for the purpose of collecting fees or protecting confidential data.

### 8. Liability

8.1 RLH Provider provides findings and recommendations based on Client's billing
data and does not guarantee any specific recovery. Client makes final billing,
pricing, and collection decisions.

8.2 To the extent permitted by law, each party's total liability under this
Agreement is limited to the fees actually paid or owed to RLH Provider under this
Agreement.
